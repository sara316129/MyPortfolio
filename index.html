01 // About (the intersection of math & aesthetics)

MATHEMATICAL LOGIC. CREATIVE PRECISION.

Bridging the theoretical frontiers of quantum computation with empirical machine learning and editorial motion graphics.

Hi, I'm Saranga, an undergraduate at the University of Colombo, Faculty of Science, pursuing a BSc (Hons) in Computational Mathematics. My academic focus is anchored in quantum computing, exploring quantum algorithms, structured circulant matrices, and their potential to overcome classical computational bottlenecks.

In parallel, I immerse myself in data modeling, pattern recognition, and predictive machine learning. Beyond traditional academics, I have a keen interest in financial markets, particularly quantitative forex trading, where I enjoy analyzing market trends, price volatility dynamics, and data-driven trading algorithms.

I am consistently driven to learn emerging technologies and apply advanced mathematics and computational modeling to solve complex, high-stakes challenges.
